How the cleaning quote price floor works

First calculate paid person-hours. Two cleaners working three hours each create six cleaning person-hours. If they are also paid for 30 minutes of travel each, add another person-hour. Multiply by your loaded labor cost, then add supplies, travel vehicle costs and a share of overhead.

Your break-even price accounts for payment processing as a percentage of the sale. A margin-target price divides total modeled job costs by one minus the payment fee rate and your desired profit margin. This is a cost-based floor, not proof customers will pay that amount.

Use this before quoting partial-home cleans or to check crew labor hours. After performing the work, compare real spending with the initial estimate as explained in our job-profit guide.

Common questions

Do two cleaners working three hours mean three labor hours?

No. They mean six person-hours for paid cleaning labor, before paid travel time.

Does this calculator know the right cleaning price in my city?

No. You control every cost and target margin. Your local market, service quality, customer requirements and competitors also affect what price is viable.

Will this save my estimates or generate branded quotes?

No. This intentionally simple free page recalculates in your browser only. The BroomProfit quoting application supports saved quotes, branded customer-facing PDF estimates and actual-versus-estimated review.
