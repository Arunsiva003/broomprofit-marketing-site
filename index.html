The quote looked fine. Then the job took longer.

Most pricing mistakes aren't about arithmetic. They're missed tasks, first-clean complexity, paid travel, overhead, and confusion between two cleaners' clock-hours and actual paid person-hours. A simple square-footage number often can't represent a partial-home scope.

BEFORE YOU BUY

Clear expectations.

Does BroomProfit send emails or take customer payments?

No. It creates printable/save-as-PDF estimates and can open an email draft. Payment collection, automated email, scheduling and invoicing are not included.

Where are my customer records stored?

Locally in the browser where you use the application. No BroomProfit account, shared cloud database or automated backup is included. Export a JSON backup often, and don't use shared devices.

Does it know cleaning prices in my city?

No. You enter your labor, travel, overhead and fee assumptions. Its purpose is cost-aware pricing discipline, not real-time market-rate recommendations.

Can multiple cleaners use it together?

It models crew size for job costing, but it's a single-device workspace. Real-time team collaboration and cross-device synchronization are not available.

Will it work without an internet connection?

The downloaded application contains no external network libraries and can run offline in a modern desktop browser. Browser security and storage behavior may vary for local files; instructions include troubleshooting.
