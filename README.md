# BroomProfit: Free US House-Cleaning Quote and Job-Profit Tools

BroomProfit offers **free, no-signup browser calculators** for independent residential cleaning businesses, including owners quoting only part of a home, first-time cleans, deep cleans and add-on services. Inputs are private to the browser. The tools use your own assumptions, **not invented local cleaning rates**.

## Free tools (open instantly)

- [Room-by-room house cleaning quote calculator](https://broomprofit-quote.netlify.app/house-cleaning-quote-calculator): choose bathrooms, bedrooms, kitchens, living areas, oven/fridge/windows and additional tasks; edit labor-time estimates; account for paid travel, crew, overhead, supplies, payment fees, and your desired margin.
- [Cleaning job profit calculator](https://broomprofit-quote.netlify.app/free-cleaning-job-profit-calculator): enter a proposed quote and expected paid person-hours to check break-even and your margin-target price floor.
- [How to estimate partial-home cleaning](https://broomprofit-quote.netlify.app/guides/partial-home-cleaning-estimate)
- [Cleaning crew paid-person-hours explained](https://broomprofit-quote.netlify.app/guides/cleaning-person-hours)
- [How to review cleaning job profit](https://broomprofit-quote.netlify.app/guides/cleaning-job-profit)
