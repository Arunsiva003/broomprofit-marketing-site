How to quote a partial-home cleaning job

Ask which rooms and add-ons the customer actually wants, and whether this is a routine or first-time deep clean. Estimate paid cleaning person-hours from the scope. Two cleaners each working two hours represent four paid person-hours, even though the visit lasts two hours.

Next account for any paid travel time, loaded labor cost, supplies, vehicle expenses and your allocated overhead. Divide these modeled costs by (1 − payment-fee percentage − desired profit-margin percentage) to estimate a target price floor. A lower quote may still make business sense for other reasons; this calculator only evaluates the cost assumptions you enter.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I charge for a first-time house cleaning?

There isn't a universal price. It depends on local wages, the home's condition, which areas are included, paid hours, supplies and the amount customers will pay. Enter your actual costs and use the resulting price floor as one decision input.

Can I use this to quote only the bathrooms and kitchen?

Yes. Set quantities of unrequested rooms to zero; leave only the selected rooms and any add-ons. You can revise time assumptions for each task before relying on the result.

Are the starting hours typical US cleaning times?

No. They are fictional editable examples. Confirm scope and condition yourself, and calibrate using completed-job labor records.
